01 / What it solves

AI that earns its place by replacing a bottleneck.

Inbound calls go to voicemail after hours.

The business runs 9 to 5, the buyers do not. Calls that come in at night get lost. We deploy voice agents that qualify, route, and book the call so nothing slips through to the next morning.

Your team is doing work AI could do.

Repeatable analysis, summarization, qualification, routing. The work is too important to skip and too expensive to keep doing by hand. We embed AI workflows that handle the volume without the headcount.

Tools have AI features your team isn't using.

The platforms your team already lives in ship with AI nobody configured. We tune what's there before we build what isn't.

02 / The Playbook

AI only pays when it replaces something that's costing you.

Most AI gets spread thin across everything and ends up as decoration. The version that pays for itself is aimed at one expensive bottleneck. We find that point first, then put AI exactly there.

01
