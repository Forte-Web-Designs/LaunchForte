01 / Where it breaks

Four places a coaching business leaks.

Your tools don't talk.

Your CRM, Kajabi, ManyChat, and your DMs each hold a piece, and none of them share it. You copy between them by hand and hope nothing falls through.

You can't see what works.

A freebie, a post, a reel, a webinar, something brings buyers. The numbers live in five places, so you're guessing which one.

You're the bottleneck.

Every sale still runs through your own DMs. You can't hire your way out, because there's no system to hand someone.

Growth breaks you.

A launch lands, the volume spikes, you drop the ball, and the month that should have climbed stalls.

It's the same business underneath, whether you're at ten thousand a month in the DMs or running launches at six figures. The leak is the system.
