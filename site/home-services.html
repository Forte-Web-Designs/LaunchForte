01 / Where it breaks

Four places a service business leaks.

Your office re-enters everything.

A job lives in your field software, your CRM, your spreadsheets, and your books, and someone keys it into all of them by hand.

Calls slip when the crew's out.

A missed call is a booked job that went to the competitor who picked up.

You can't see which leads pay.

Modernize, Angi, Google, referrals, the leads come from everywhere, but you can't tell which source brings the jobs worth having.

Growth breaks the office.

More trucks and more jobs, and the manual scheduling and follow up can't keep up, so things fall through and the repeat business dries up.

It's the same business underneath, whether you run two trucks or twenty. The leak is the system.
