03 / How it works

From setup to a running calendar.

The system runs in three phases. Setup gets the machine in place. Launch gets it sending. Steady state is when the calendar fills on its own.

Weeks 1 to 2: setup.

We map your ideal customer, build the targeting, write the message, set up the infrastructure that keeps the outbound deliverable. Inbox warming, sending domains, tracking. Nothing goes out until the foundation is right.

Weeks 3 to 4: launch and tune.

The system goes live. Volume starts low and ramps as the data comes back. We watch reply rates, booked calls, qualification quality. Iteration is in the build, not bolted on after.

Month 2 and beyond: steady state.

The calendar starts moving. The data sharpens the targeting and the messaging. The system gets better the longer it runs. Most clients see their first qualified booked calls in the first 30 to 60 days.
