Common Questions

What buyers usually ask before booking an audit.

What's the difference between Launch Forte and a typical automation agency?

Most automation agencies install tools. We diagnose first, architect the system, and engineer it to survive volume. The deliverable isn't an automation. It's infrastructure that holds when traffic doubles.

Why do most engagements start with an audit?

Most quotes are guesses. The audit is three days, $1,500, and produces a written diagnostic with prioritized recommendations and fixed pricing. It credits toward your first build over $3,000.

How is pricing structured?

Three tiers. Audit is $1,500. Builds are fixed price after audit, scoped from the diagnostic. Partnership starts at $3,500 per month, six month minimum.

Who do you typically work with?

Founders, operators, and growth leaders running businesses that have outgrown the tools that got them here. Some engagements run alongside marketing strategists. Others put us in the strategy seat. The audit decides which shape fits.

Where are you located and who do you serve?

Grapevine, Texas, in the Dallas Fort Worth metro. Clients across the United States, mostly remote. Local DFW operators occasionally meet in person.
