What We Build

Five categories of engineering work.

Every system on this page started as a problem a real business was paying for in time, revenue, or both. The five categories below are how we organize the work. The audit is how we figure out which one matters most for your business right now.

01 / Category

Revenue Infrastructure

The foundation everything else runs on.

CRM nobody configured. Pipeline stages that don't match how the team sells. Payment integration that stops at Stripe. Data architecture so fragmented that reporting requires a spreadsheet. We build the operational backbone that makes everything else work, configured around how your business operates instead of how the platform documentation says to run it.

What we typically build

  • Full CRM configuration with pipeline stages, tags, source attribution, and data hygiene
  • Payment integration with conversion tracking back to CRM and ad platforms
  • Email deliverability infrastructure (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, warmup, A2P 10DLC compliance)
  • Client onboarding automation that fires the right next step on payment, signup, or contract sign
  • Multi platform pipeline migration (Kajabi, Infusionsoft, ClickFunnels, Mailchimp to GoHighLevel or target platform)
  • Custom field architecture and webhook integrations between platforms
Recent example

"Built a complete GHL infrastructure for an education brand that was running on Kajabi with no CRM. Pipeline stages mapped to their actual sales process, Stripe with three price points, calendar with Zoom integration, full email authentication. The brand grew from $40K to $160K monthly in the three months after the foundation was in place. We didn't grow the brand. We built the infrastructure that let the strategy land."

02 / Category

Lead and Customer Systems

Capture demand. Convert it. Don't let it leak.

Forms that sit unanswered for hours. Missed calls that go to voicemail. Booked appointments with no reminder so half the calendar evaporates. Dead leads in the CRM nobody has the bandwidth to wake up. We build the systems that capture demand you've already paid for and convert it before it leaks.

What we typically build

  • Instant response systems with under 60 second SMS, email, and CRM logging on every form submission and missed call
  • Sales funnels with landing pages, opt ins, behavior triggered nurture sequences, and CRM integration
  • Lead routing with rules based assignment, hot lead alerts, and full context handoff
  • Database reactivation campaigns with segmentation, AI personalized outreach, and reply handling
  • Review automation with sentiment gating, Google integration, and private feedback flows
  • Webinar funnels with paid registration, attendance tracking, dual CTA post-webinar pages, and pipeline automation
  • Dispatch systems for field service with GPS verification, dynamic pricing, and proof gates
Recent example

"Built the full webinar funnel infrastructure for a real estate education company running paid live webinars. $27 entry, $7,000 product on the back end with a fast action $5,000 offer. Stripe with three price points wired into the pipeline, Zoom integration, attendance tracking, dual CTA post-webinar pages, and full pipeline automation that moves contacts through stages without anyone touching it. The fractional CMO running the marketing strategy needed the engineering to run. That's what we built."

03 / Category

AI Agents and Orchestration

AI deployed where it earns its place.

Most AI implementations in 2026 are decoration. The AI work that survives is the AI work that earns its place. Voice agents that qualify callers. Chatbots configured with the real sales process. Custom AI workflow tools built around how your team operates. We build AI where the math works. We don't build AI where it doesn't, even if the client asks for it.

What we typically build

  • Custom AI voice agents on Retell, Voiceflow, or platform native (GoHighLevel, ElevenLabs)
  • AI website chatbots configured with knowledge base, sales process, and escalation rules
  • AI workflow tools (custom GPTs, Google Workspace Gems, Claude skill files) for internal team operations
  • AI personalized nurture sequences with brand voice enforcement and anti hallucination guardrails
  • AI configured into platforms you already run (GoHighLevel AI, HubSpot AI, etc.)
  • AI orchestration across multi step workflows (transcription, classification, routing, summary, action)
Recent example

"Built an AI content engine for a consulting brand. Long form Substack article goes in, AI processes it through brand voice rules and platform constraints, drafts come out for LinkedIn, X, Threads, Instagram, and Substack Notes. Routed to a Notion content dashboard with pipeline stages. The strategy was hers. The voice rules were hers. We built the engine that runs them at scale."

04 / Category

Reporting and Dashboards

Decisions from real numbers, not gut feel.

The data exists in five tools and doesn't agree across them. The team builds Monday morning reports by hand because nobody trusts the dashboards. We build automated reporting that pulls from the actual sources, normalizes the data, and surfaces the metrics that drive decisions. The dashboard is downstream of the data architecture. The architecture decides whether the dashboard is trustworthy.

What we typically build

  • Real time pipeline dashboards with leads by source, response times, booking rates, and conversion stage
  • Ad spend attribution from campaign through closed deal across Meta, Google, TikTok, and more
  • Operational dashboards for service businesses (jobs, payouts, completion rates, team performance)
  • Founder dashboards consolidating financial, operational, and pipeline metrics in one view
  • KPI frameworks built around your business model (subscription, transactional, services, hybrid)
  • Automated weekly and monthly reporting with delivery to email or Slack
Recent example

"Built a multi client ad spend reporting system for a marketing agency in Ireland. Automated monthly pulls from Google Ads and Meta into Google Sheets, billing calculations for five clients with different fee structures (flat fee plus percentage, percentage with caps, mixed), routed into client invoices. Replaced a manual process that was eating one full day every month per client."

05 / Category

Custom Builds and Integrations

When the off the shelf answer doesn't fit.

Some of the most valuable engineering work doesn't fit a template. Field service businesses that need offline capable apps. Family offices that need financial data pipelines with complex authentication. Operations that need agentic systems no off the shelf platform builds. We work in React, Vite, n8n, Python, and the broader stack you'd expect from a software engineer.

What we typically build

  • Standalone web applications (React, Vite, PWA with offline support)
  • Financial data integration pipelines (Plaid, SnapTrade, banking APIs with HMAC, OAuth, custom auth)
  • Dispatch systems with GPS verification, dynamic pricing, and proof gates for field service
  • Operations hubs with multi tier payouts, commission calculations, and approval workflows
  • Custom API integrations between platforms (Salesforce to custom backend, Shopify to Xero, etc.)
  • Progressive Web Apps replacing legacy software (Microsoft Access, paper-based processes, manual workflows)
Recent example

"Built a Progressive Web App for a moving company replacing three legacy systems (a custom calculator, a Microsoft Access database, and a paper based contract system). Client info entry, room by room inventory with pricing, estimate calculation with multiple modifiers (distance, second truck, load only), 11 contract types generated as PDFs, full Google Sheets and Drive integration, works offline during walkthroughs in basements and signal dead zones."
