01 / Where n8n fits

Built for workflows with branching logic.

n8n suits workflows that need conditional logic or custom code, or that handle data you'd rather not route through a third party's servers. For a simple two step connection, Make or Zapier is often enough. We'll tell you which you need before we build anything. The three are compared in more detail on sethforte.com.

02 / What we build

Workflows for the work that repeats.

If it happens the same way every week, it shouldn't need a person to remember it.

Lead handling and routing.

New inquiries are captured, assigned, and answered on a schedule you set.

Billing and collections.

Invoices and payment reminders move on their own, with a record of what was sent.

Reporting.

Scheduled pulls from your systems into the report you already read.

Internal approvals.

Requests reach the right person and wait for a decision. No chasing.

Accounting sync.

A closed deal in your CRM creates the invoice in QuickBooks, and payments write back so both systems agree. See QuickBooks and Xero integration.

03 / Keeping it running

A workflow can run green and still do nothing.

The failure we see most is an automation that reports success while the work never happened. We add checks that confirm the outcome, and alerts that reach a person when it didn't. We wrote about it in catching failures nobody notices.

06 / Common questions

What teams ask before building on n8n.

Cloud or self hosted?

Either. We'll recommend one based on where your data should live and who will maintain it.

Do you build on Make or Zapier too?

Yes, when they fit better. We match the tool to the workflow, not the other way around.

Where does a project start?

With a Growth Systems Audit, a written diagnostic of your workflows and what each fix would take.

Do you connect n8n to QuickBooks and HubSpot?

Yes. n8n has connections for QuickBooks Online and HubSpot, and a common job is a closed deal in the CRM creating the invoice in the accounting system. Where a connection is missing, we use the system's API.

Does it work with Microsoft 365 and Outlook?

Yes. Mail, calendar and Teams can start a workflow or receive its results. If your company already pays for Microsoft 365, we'll tell you whether Power Automate or n8n fits the job better.
