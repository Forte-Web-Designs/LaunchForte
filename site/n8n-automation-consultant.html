n8n Automation

n8n workflows built to keep running after launch.

Building and monitoring n8n workflows for companies anywhere in the US.

01 / Where n8n fits

A good fit for workflows with branching logic.

n8n suits workflows that need conditional logic or custom code, or that handle data you would rather not route through a third party's servers. For a simple two step connection, Make or Zapier is often enough, and I'll tell you which you need before I build anything. I compare the three in more detail on sethforte.com.

02 / What I build

Workflows for the work that repeats.

Lead handling and routing

New inquiries are captured and assigned, then answered on a schedule you set.

Billing and collections

Invoices and payment reminders move on their own, with a record of what was sent.

Reporting

Scheduled pulls from your systems into the report you already read.

Internal approvals

Requests reach the right person and wait for a decision without chasing.

03 / Keeping it running

A workflow can run green and still do nothing.

The failure I see most is an automation that reports success while the work never happened. I add checks that confirm the outcome and alerts that reach a person when it didn't. I wrote about that in catching failures nobody notices.
