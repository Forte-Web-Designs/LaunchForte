CRM integration

CRM integration across Dallas-Fort Worth.

I connect your CRM to the rest of your business so a lead, a quote and a payment are one record, not three.

The problem

A CRM that stands alone.

A CRM that nobody trusts usually has one thing in common: it does not talk to billing, scheduling or the inbox, so people keep a second copy in a spreadsheet. Once that happens, reports disagree and follow-up slips.

Integration fixes the cause. Contacts, deals, appointments and invoices move between systems automatically, with rules for which system wins when two disagree.

What I connect

The systems most teams already own.

HubSpot, GoHighLevel and other CRMs, QuickBooks and Xero, Stripe, Shopify, Calendly, email and SMS tools, forms, and internal databases. The work is the connections, the field mapping, the failure handling and the monitoring, not a new platform for its own sake.

Sometimes the right answer is a setting you already have. I will tell you that rather than build something you do not need.

How we work together

Scoped first, then built.

Based in Grapevine, Texas, working with DFW and U.S. businesses remotely. I start with an audit of how data moves today, then give you a fixed scope and price. Testing uses your real records, and you keep the accounts and documentation.
