Accounting Integrations

QuickBooks and Xero, connected to everything else.

Integration and automation for the accounting side of a business, built remotely from Grapevine.

The problem What we build Examples Controls Questions

01 / The problem

The same job, typed into two systems.

When the CRM or the field software can't talk to the accounting system, someone retypes every job into it. The two drift apart over time, and month end turns into a hunt for the difference.

02 / What we build

Four ways the books stay in step.

Each one removes a step someone is doing by hand today.

Job or order to invoice.

A closed job or a new order creates the invoice without anyone keying it in.

Payments and collections.

Payments are matched to invoices, and late ones get a follow up.

Reconciliation.

Payments and the ledger are checked against each other, with every difference listed.

Reporting.

Figures come from the accounting system itself, on a schedule.

03 / Examples

Builds we can point to.

Accounting work, done for real businesses.

Shopify to Xero.

A furniture retailer lost the third party integration that moved its products from Shopify into Xero. We built the replacement sync. Read the case study.

Capital calls in QuickBooks.

An investment group needed capital call payments tracked and reconciled in QuickBooks. We built the automation that does it.

Collections in Power Automate.

A recruiting firm needed its collections follow up handled without a person keeping a list. We built it in Power Automate.

04 / Controls

Systems that touch money need guardrails.

We build in approvals and logs, so you can review what ran and when. We wrote about the approach in automating financial operations safely.

05 / How we work

Remote, from Grapevine.

We're based in Grapevine, Texas. We work with companies across Dallas-Fort Worth and the rest of the US by video call and shared screens. We don't visit client offices.

You own the accounts.

Everything is built inside your own accounts, under your login. Nothing held hostage.

You own the code and the documentation.

Every system is documented and handed over. You can run it yourself, hand it to a team, or have us keep extending it. Your call.

06 / Common questions

What owners ask before connecting the books.

What access do you need?

Only what the integration requires, granted through your own accounts. You can remove it whenever you want.

Which accounting systems do you work with?

QuickBooks and Xero are the ones on this page. If you use something else, tell us and we'll say whether we can help.

Where does a project start?

With a Growth Systems Audit, a written diagnostic of how your systems connect and what we would fix first.

Can QuickBooks connect to HubSpot or another CRM?

Yes. A won deal can create the customer and the invoice in QuickBooks, and payment status can flow back to the CRM so sales sees what has been paid.
