Accounting Integrations

QuickBooks and Xero, connected to everything else.

Integration and automation for the accounting side of a business, built remotely from Grapevine.

01 / The problem

The same job, typed into two systems.

When the CRM or the field software can't talk to the accounting system, someone retypes each job into it, and the two drift apart over time.

02 / What I build

Four ways accounting stays in step.

Job or order to invoice

A closed job or a new order creates the invoice without anyone keying it in.

Payments and collections

Payments are matched to invoices and late ones get a follow up.

Reconciliation

Payments and the ledger are checked against each other, with differences listed.

Reporting

Figures come from the accounting system itself, on a schedule.

03 / Examples

Builds I can point to.

  • A furniture retailer lost the third party integration that moved its products from Shopify into Xero, so I built the replacement sync. Read the case study.
  • An investment group needed capital call payments tracked and reconciled in QuickBooks, and I built the automation that does it.
  • A recruiting firm needed its collections follow-up handled without a person keeping a list, and I built it in Power Automate.

04 / Controls

Systems that touch money need guardrails.

I build in approvals and logs so you can review what ran. I wrote about the approach in automating financial operations safely.

05 / How I work

Remote, from Grapevine.

I'm based in Grapevine, Texas, and I work with companies across Dallas-Fort Worth and the rest of the US by video call and shared screens. I don't visit client offices. When the build is done you own the accounts and the code, and I hand over the documentation.
