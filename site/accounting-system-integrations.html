Accounting integrations

QuickBooks and Xero integrations.

I connect your accounting system to your orders, payments and CRM so the books update without re-keying.

The problem

Re-keying is where errors start.

When invoices, payments and orders are entered twice, the totals drift. Someone then spends days reconciling instead of running the business.

An integration moves the data once, checks that the systems agree, and flags the exceptions for a person.

What I build

Sync, reconciliation and collections.

Order and payment sync from Shopify and Stripe into QuickBooks or Xero, invoice creation from CRM deals, automated payment reminders and collections follow-up, and reconciliation checks that show where two systems disagree.

Past work includes a QuickBooks payment and reconciliation workflow for an investment group, a Shopify-to-Xero order sync for a retailer, and collections automation for a recruiting firm.

Working together

Careful with financial data.
