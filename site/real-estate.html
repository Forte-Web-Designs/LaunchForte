06 / Common questions

What agents ask before booking.

Do you specialize in real estate, or are you a generalist?

GoHighLevel systems for solo operators are a core focus, and real estate runs on exactly that machinery, speed to lead, buyer and seller pipelines, and long term follow up. We build and document the system so it runs the way a producing agent needs, on autopilot.

Do I need GoHighLevel, or can you work with my current CRM?

Both. We build inside GoHighLevel, or we build on and connect your existing CRM, ex: Follow Up Boss or kvCORE, and if you want to move to GoHighLevel we migrate you cleanly with your full contact history intact.

Can you handle A2P 10DLC and SMS compliance?

Yes. Texting leads compliantly requires A2P 10DLC registration and a verified sending setup, and that is part of every build, so your CRM can text, call, and email from day one.

Will I be able to run it myself after?

Yes. Every build is handed off with a walkthrough video and a written SOP, and you own the account and everything in it. The ongoing partnership is there if you want it, never required.

Do you build from scratch or use a snapshot?

We configure a proven structure to your business and your market, which is faster than starting from zero and gives you a clean, maintainable system rather than a generic template you have to fight.
