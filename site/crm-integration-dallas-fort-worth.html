CRM Integration

Connect your CRM to the rest of your business.

CRM setup and integration for companies in Dallas-Fort Worth and across the US, built remotely from Grapevine.

01 / The problem

A CRM is only as useful as the data that reaches it.

A CRM that holds half the picture gets abandoned. Leads arrive through forms and DMs that never make it in, and invoices live in accounting software the CRM can't see.

02 / What I build

Four pieces of a CRM that people use.

Setup that matches how you sell

Pipelines and fields built around your actual sales process instead of a template.

Lead capture

Forms, calls, texts and DMs routed into the CRM, with the source of each lead recorded.

Billing and accounting sync

Invoices and payments agree between the CRM and QuickBooks or Xero.

Reporting

Dashboards that read from the systems themselves, so no one rebuilds a spreadsheet each week.

03 / Your CRM

I work with the CRM you already have.

If you're choosing one, I'll tell you which fits how you sell. I've written up the decision for coaches and course creators and for home service businesses. If the CRM itself is what's holding you back, the audit will say so.

04 / Integration work

Examples of connecting systems.

  • A furniture retailer lost the third party integration that moved its products from Shopify into Xero, so I built the replacement sync. Read the case study.
  • An investment group needed capital call payments tracked and reconciled in QuickBooks, and I built the automation that does it.

The accounting side has its own page: QuickBooks and Xero integration.

05 / How I work

Remote, from Grapevine.

I'm based in Grapevine, Texas, and I work with companies across Dallas-Fort Worth and the rest of the US by video call and shared screens. I don't visit client offices. When the build is done you own the accounts and the code, and I hand over the documentation.
