01 / The problem

A CRM is only as useful as the data that reaches it.

A CRM that holds half the picture gets abandoned. Leads arrive through forms and DMs that never make it in. Invoices live in accounting software the CRM can't see. So the team goes back to the spreadsheet, and the CRM becomes one more bill.

02 / What we build

Four pieces of a CRM people actually use.

Each piece is built around how your business sells, and each one is yours once it ships.

Setup that matches how you sell.

Pipelines and fields built around your actual sales process, not a template.

Lead capture.

Forms, calls, texts and DMs routed into the CRM, with the source of every lead recorded.

Billing and accounting sync.

Invoices and payments agree between the CRM and QuickBooks or Xero. No retyping.

Reporting.

Dashboards that read from the systems themselves, so no one rebuilds a spreadsheet each week.

03 / Your CRM

We work with the CRM you already have.

If you're choosing one, we'll tell you which fits how you sell. We've written up the decision for coaches and course creators and for home service businesses. If the CRM itself is what's holding you back, the audit will say so.

05 / How we work

Remote, from Grapevine.

We're based in Grapevine, Texas. We work with companies across Dallas-Fort Worth and the rest of the US by video call and shared screens. We don't visit client offices.

You own the accounts.

Everything is built inside your own accounts, under your login. Nothing held hostage.

You own the code and the documentation.

Every system is documented and handed over. You can run it yourself, hand it to a team, or have us keep extending it. Your call.

06 / Common questions

What owners ask before connecting the CRM.

Do you replace our CRM?

Only when the CRM is what's holding things back. Otherwise we connect it to the tools around it.

Do you work with companies outside Dallas-Fort Worth?

Yes. The work is remote, so location doesn't limit it.

Where does a project start?

With a Growth Systems Audit, a written diagnostic that maps your systems and lists what we would fix first.

Do you integrate HubSpot?

Yes. We connect HubSpot to QuickBooks and Xero, to your forms and calendar, and to the other tools around it, so deals, invoices and payments agree. We'll say first whether HubSpot's own integrations already cover it.

Does the CRM connect to Microsoft 365 and Outlook?

Yes. Email and calendar activity can reach the CRM, and a request that arrives by email can become a task or a deal. We build it with Power Automate or n8n, depending on what you license.
