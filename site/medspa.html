06 / Common questions

What owners ask before booking.

Do you only do reactivation?

No. Reactivation is where many med spas start because it pays for itself fast, but we build the whole patient system, new patient capture, booking, retention, and reporting, and run it with you.

Do I need new software?

No. We build inside the system you already run, ex: Boulevard, Mangomint, or Zenoti. Your team keeps working where they know, and you own everything when we're done.

How do you handle patient data?

Carefully. A patient list is protected health information, so every engagement that touches it includes a signed BAA, consent handling, and texting rules set to your state. The compliance is handled before anything sends.

Where should we start?

Usually the Patient Reactivation Campaign. It is fixed price, low risk, and the report it produces shows exactly what to build next.

Is there a contract or retainer to start?

No. The campaign is a single fixed fee, no retainer and no contract. The build and the ongoing partnership come later, and only if you want them.
