Business automation

Business automation for DFW companies.

I build the workflows that move leads, orders and paperwork between your systems, so your team stops doing it by hand.

What I build

Workflows that carry the work.

Most businesses I talk to are not missing software. They have a CRM, an accounting tool, a scheduler and a spreadsheet, and a person who copies information between them every day. I replace that copying with automation that runs on its own and tells someone when it fails.

Typical work: lead intake and follow-up, appointment and quote handoffs, invoice and payment reconciliation, reporting that updates itself, and the monitoring that tells you when something stops.

How it works

Remote, fixed scope, documented.

Based in Grapevine, Texas, working with DFW and U.S. businesses remotely. Everything happens over video and shared documents, so there is no travel and nothing to schedule around. Work starts with a Growth Systems Audit that maps what you run today. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything is built, and you own the accounts, the code and the documentation.

Every build is tested against your real cases before it goes live, and I stay on through the first weeks to fix what real data shows.
