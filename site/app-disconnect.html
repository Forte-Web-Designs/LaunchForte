Disconnecting a Launch Forte integration

If you connected a Launch Forte integration to your QuickBooks Online company or another platform and want to disconnect it, you have two options, and both take effect immediately.

Option 1: revoke access in the platform

In QuickBooks Online, sign in as an admin, open the Apps area, choose Manage Apps, find the Launch Forte app, and choose Disconnect. This immediately revokes the integration's access to your company data. Other platforms have an equivalent connected apps or integrations page where access can be revoked the same way.

Option 2: ask us

Email seth@launchforte.com and ask us to disconnect. We will revoke the connection from our side and confirm in writing.

What happens after disconnecting

The integration immediately loses the ability to read or write data in your account. Automations that depended on the connection stop running. Data retention after disconnection follows our Privacy Policy at launchforte.com/privacy. Disconnecting does not delete anything inside your own platforms; your QuickBooks data, for example, remains exactly as it is in your company file.

If you disconnect by mistake, the connection can be restored by re-authorizing the app.
