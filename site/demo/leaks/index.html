Leak Finder

Free revenue leak scan

Find revenue slipping between your jobs and your invoices.

Drop two exports, your job or estimate list and your invoice list. In under a minute you get a report that names the completed jobs that were never invoiced and the invoices that came in under estimate, with the math on every number and an annualized figure if the pattern holds.

Built for services businesses and contractors. This is a diagnostic, not accounting advice. It points at jobs and invoices worth a second look; you confirm what is real.
