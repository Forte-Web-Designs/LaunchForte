Fields watched
Every field, watched

This diff only checks the two scrapes pasted above. Fields are matched by label between yesterday's snapshot and today's, and anything whose value changed gets a verdict. Money and access fields — price, phone, booking link, contact endpoint, SSL status — are marked critical when they move, because those are the ones that cost a call or a quote. Discoverability and presentation fields — title, meta description, hours, address, category, headline — are marked worth a look. A handful of fields (footer year, asset version, internal timestamp) are always marked fine, because they're supposed to change on every scrape. It does not know whether a change was authorized, does not screenshot anything, and cannot see anything that isn't in the text you pasted.
