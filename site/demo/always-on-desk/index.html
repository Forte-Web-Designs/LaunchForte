How the audit works

Three checks, run against your own documents.

Not a vibe check. Every answer is compared to the real source text, word for word.

1

Is there a source at all?

Any answer with no citation is flagged immediately. Confidence isn't proof.

2

Does the source exist and say that?

Citations to documents that aren't in the knowledge base, or that don't contain the fact claimed, get caught.

3

Is the source still current?

A correct quote from a retired policy is still telling customers the wrong thing.
