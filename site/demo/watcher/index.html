Why this matters

Silent failures are the worst of it.

A crashed page gets reported fast. A quiet sync runs dark for weeks while the numbers drift.

A signup flow fired on empty email addresses thousands of times before anyone noticed. The form looked fine. The list was garbage.
A tagging bug silently mislabeled new contacts for weeks. Every automation downstream of the tag ran on the wrong people.
A server locked itself mid upgrade on a Friday night. The first report came from a customer on Monday.

Customers are your alerting system, until something else is. The stopwatch above is the gap between your press and the alert email. On real production systems, the same watcher pattern catches these silent failures before they cost you money.
