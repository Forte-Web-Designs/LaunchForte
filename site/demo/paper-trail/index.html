How the audit works

Three checks, run against your own records.

Not a vibe check. Every contract and proposal is traced from generated, to signed, to filed, to stamped.

1

Was it actually signed?

Sent-but-pending, declined, voided, or missing from the signing system entirely all get caught here.

2

Was the signed copy filed?

A signed PDF sitting in an inbox is not the same thing as a filed contract. This checks for both.

3

Does the audit trail hold up?

Missing stamps and timestamps that don't line up both mean the trail wouldn't survive a challenge.
