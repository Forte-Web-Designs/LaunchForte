How the ranking works

Three steps, run against your own applicant pile.

Not a gut feeling. Every applicant is checked against the bar you actually set, line by line.

Minimum experience, commute radius, shift, a required license, a pay ceiling — whatever you'd actually screen for.

Check every applicant against it
