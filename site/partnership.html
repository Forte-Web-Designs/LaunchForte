Embedded Growth Systems Partner

We become the engineering brain behind your growth motion.

Most engagements end at handoff. The build is delivered, the documentation is written, and we step away. Some clients want the opposite. They want us in the business, not at the door. The Embedded Growth Systems Partner is for those clients.

What's included

What's included

Monthly Strategy Review

60 minutes every month. We look at what's working, what's not, and what's next. The data, the bottlenecks, the next system to build, the next risk to manage.

Async Access

Loom videos, voice memos, and written answers to tactical questions between sessions. If a 10 minute response saves you a week of figuring it out, that's the win.

Ongoing Optimization

Up to 4 hours per month of small fixes, tweaks, and adjustments. Not new builds. The little things that come up when systems run in the real world.

Priority on New Builds

When you scope a new project with us, you get retainer client pricing and front of the line scheduling.

What this is not

What this is not

Not a managed service

If you want us to run your day to day operations, that's a different engagement. The Partnership is for businesses with their own team running the system who need a strategic engineering partner above the execution.

Not a build retainer

If you need 20 hours of new development this month, that's a project. Scope it separately. The Partnership covers small adjustments and strategic input, not new builds at retainer rates.

Not a consulting engagement

Consultants come in, deliver a recommendation, and leave. We stay. We're in the business with you for the engagement length.

Who this is for

Who this is for

Owners who built a system with us in Phase 1 and are scaling. Teams that don't need another builder, they need a strategic engineering partner at the table. Businesses where the next mistake will cost more than a year of this retainer.

Engagement structure

Engagement structure

From $3,500 per month. Six month minimum. Auto renewing month to month after that, cancel with 30 days notice. Most retainer clients start with a Growth Systems Audit or Core Systems Build before moving into the Partnership. The Partnership picks up where the build ends.

Start With An Audit

Most Partnerships start with a Growth Systems Audit. We map your infrastructure end to end and identify what to build first. The audit is how we both know whether the Partnership is the right next step.
