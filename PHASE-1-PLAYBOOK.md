## Voice guardrails for any future copy in these directories
Match the rest of the site:
- No emojis, em dashes, en dashes, double hyphens, arrows, tildes
- No hyphens between compound words (use "follow up" not "follow-up")
- Banned words: transformed, mastered, leverage, streamline, robust, seamless, transformative, compelling, innovative, foster, cultivate, navigate, unlock, actually
- "ex:" not "e.g."
- Plain language, confident, warm but direct
