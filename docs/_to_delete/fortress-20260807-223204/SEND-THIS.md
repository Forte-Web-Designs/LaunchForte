## When their tool isn't in the folder

Send the same four. Swap beat 3 for whichever tool you do have, and say the substitution
out loud — never let the proposal imply it's their tool:

> *"That build is in GoHighLevel and this one's in HubSpot, because that's where my last
> two clients lived. The tool changes; the architecture doesn't — stage criteria, a
> time-based watcher, an escalation path and a fail-safe are the same four pieces in yours."*

Naming it reads as confidence. Hiding it reads as a bait-and-switch the moment they notice.

On Upwork, **attach the files** — do not link to launchforte.com. Links risk reading as circumvention.
