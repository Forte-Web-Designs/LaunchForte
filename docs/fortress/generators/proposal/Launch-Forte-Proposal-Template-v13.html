The working agreement, short version

Fixed price, funded a milestone at a time, net-15 on acceptance. The number moves only when the scope moves, agreed in writing first.

Each milestone includes 2 revision rounds against the written spec, and an adjustment that adds no new outcome is absorbed at no charge.

Each delivery has a 10 business day window for specific written objections, and then the next milestone begins.

A blocking item on your side extends the timeline day for day, and at 7 business days idle the project pauses.

[SUPPORT DAYS] days of support follow go live, with anything missing the spec fixed there at no charge. [SUPPORT PLAN LINE]

Everything delivered and paid for is yours. You stay owner and admin of every account, with named user access and no shared passwords.

Either side can end with written notice. Delivered milestones are yours, unstarted milestones are never owed, and anything I cannot deliver is refunded.

Third party tools stay subscribed in your name, with their costs and their terms staying yours.
