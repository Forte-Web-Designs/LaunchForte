graded postings in `upwork_jobs` — the ones the judge said are worth bidding —
by what the buyer actually asks for rather than which tool they name.

## Sample data, everywhere

One invented business per scenario family, carried across every tool so the
screenshots read as one estate rather than a pile of demos:

- **Meridian Paper Co.** — the planner brand. Storefront, upsell, waitlist,
  abandoned cart, winback. Products: Weekly Planner, Undated Planner, Desk Pad,
  Pen Set, Sticker Pack, Refill Insert, Cover Sleeve.
- **Halden Restoration** — the contractor. Quotes, stalled deals, scheduling,
  no-shows, invoice chasing, field reporting.
- **Brightline Moving** — the mover. Intake, speed to lead, crew scheduling,
  review requests.
- **Cassia Health Group** — the multi-location clinic. Booking, reminders,
  no-show recovery, compliance.
- **Ordway Supply** — the wholesaler. Inventory, two-way sync, reconciliation,
  approval routing.

Every contact is `first.last@example.com`. No real names, no real companies,
nothing Upwork-related anywhere in frame, never a credential, never a billing
page.
