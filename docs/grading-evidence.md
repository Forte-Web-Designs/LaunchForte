# Grading Evidence

Acceptance criteria for each task are now written by the AI directly from the
job instruction, not authored or curated by a human beforehand.

The reviewing agent grades those AI-written criteria against the real commit
diff produced for the task — not against a description, a plan, or a summary
of the work.

This means grading reflects what actually changed in the repository, and the
criteria being graded are generated from the same instruction the work was
based on.
